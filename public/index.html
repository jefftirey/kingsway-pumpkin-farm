Growing family memories

Kingsway Pumpkin Farm has been growing pumpkins and fall memories in Hartville, Ohio since 1990. A family-friendly experience, free from the dark side of Halloween.

Hours & Admission

Open seven days a week during the season.

Monday – Saturday  10:00 am – 7:00 pm
Sunday  1:00 – 6:00 pm
Last admission 30 minutes before close.
$12

General Admission

Per person. Ages 3 and under are free. Includes the wagon ride, farm animals, corn maze, straw tunnel, corn box, pedal karts and more.

$10

Monday – Thursday

Per person. Weekday discount on general admission.

$2

Kids Barrel Train

Per ride, for riders 48" and under. Not included in admission.

  • Pumpkins are sold separately, by the pound. About 35 cents per pound depending on variety.
  • Wagon rides run Saturdays and Sundays in September and daily starting October 1, weather permitting. Check Facebook for updates.
  • Cash gladly accepted with no fee. A 3% fee applies to all credit card transactions.
